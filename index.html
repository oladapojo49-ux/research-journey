Mentoring and Research Writing among Postgraduate Students in Nigeria

Olu Adeyemi & Fatima Bello

ABSTRACT. This study examined how structured mentoring shapes research writing among postgraduate students. Using a mixed-methods design with 240 students across four universities, it found that students who received weekly feedback produced stronger literature reviews and identified research gaps faster. However, few studies have tested such programmes in Nigerian universities, which limits what we know about local contexts.

Keywords: mentoring; research writing; postgraduate education; Nigeria

CHAPTER ONE

INTRODUCTION

1.1 Background to the Study

Research writing is a core skill for every postgraduate student, yet many struggle to identify a clear research gap (Adeyemi & Bello, 2023). Structured mentoring has been shown to improve literature reviews and shorten the time to proposal approval (Mensah, 2022). However, few studies have tested such programmes in Nigerian universities. This study therefore examines how a 12-week mentoring programme improves research writing among postgraduate students in Nigeria.
